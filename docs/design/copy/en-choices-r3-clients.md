## Decided while drawing
1. **Money** (changed in the consistency pass, see below): booking prices are booking information — Owner, Admin and Specialist see them in Upcoming and History. Sums are See revenue, Owner only: the list’s “Average check” column, the card’s visit total and average check.
2. **Specialist’s clients** (Round 3.2: she now sees only her own bookings on them, see below) = everyone with any booking with her — past, upcoming or a request (Mahym D.’s unanswered request yesterday counts): 41, page of 12. She sees the same row data (also next bookings with others) and the full card except the sums; she may add notes and remove reports only on her own bookings within 7 days; no Add client, Change, Phone only switch or Delete. Another specialist’s client by URL → the no-access state.
3. **Tags:** Reported = has at least one report (Gülälek N. is Reported because of her 19 August No-show); Regular / New / Lapsed by the spec rules; custom tags (VIP, Child) stay, after the system tags. Counts: Owner All 148 · Regular 38 · New 9 · Lapsed 27 · Reported 3; Specialist 41 · 14 · 3 · 6 · 2.
4. **Last visit** (Round 3.2: now the last *completed* visit, see below) = the last booking that took place (Completed or No-show); a visit in progress doesn’t count (Bahar A. 6 Oct; Gülälek N. 28 September — B-Calendar’s drawer line says “Today”, a drift there). Default sort Last visit, newest first; a client just added stays on top, highlighted.
5. **Next booking** = the earliest upcoming booking or request; a waiting request (or time change) shows only the timer pill in the row, the badge stays for the card and the modal.
6. **Delete:** Owner and Admin, from the row menu and the card. Blocked by any upcoming booking or waiting request; the rows link to the booking (calendar) or the request (bookings list); a time change counts with its booking. The allowed copy says past bookings stay in the calendar without the name; nothing about reports.
7. **Ýeral account** (corrected in Round 3.2): a client record links to a Ýeral account when someone signs in to Ýeral with that phone number — whether the client booked online or by phone. Gülälek, Ogulnabat and Aýjemal have signed in; Döwlet B.’s number (his mother’s) hasn’t, so it stays editable. The pill “Ýeral account” sits next to the number so staff see why it’s locked; the read-only caption is the brief’s.
8. **Phone only:** can also be turned on by hand; turning it off shows the reset line (Round 3.2: only when the no-show count turned it on); removing the No-show that caused it lifts it (count back to 1, no reset line), as round 2 §4 says. Removing a No-show also turns that history row back to Completed and recalculates visits, total and average check.
9. Each report keeps its own 7 days from when it was made; older rows show no button. The Specialist sees every report on her client’s card.
10. ~~Removed the reminder-SMS switch~~ → Round 3.2: the switch is back as “Don’t send reminders”.
11. ~~A client saved without a name shows the phone number as the name.~~ → Round 3.2: “Unnamed client” with the number below.
12. One gradient button per view: the empty state’s Add client is secondary; New booking hides while editing.
13.
