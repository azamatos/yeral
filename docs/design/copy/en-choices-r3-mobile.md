- **“Last visit today” / “Last visit 13 Oct”** · third line of a phone client row. The desktop has a Last visit column; on phones the label goes in front of the value. A No-show is marked after it, using the same “No-show” marker and icon as the desktop.

- **“Next”** (label) + day + time · right side of a phone client row, as in the desktop’s Next column. A waiting request adds its timer pill: “21 min”, title “Request · 21 min left to answer”.

- **“Gülälek N. · today · 11:30”** · subtitle of the Report sheet, naming the booking it reports.
- **aria “More · Settings is here” / “More · Reviews is here”** · the More tab while Settings or Reviews is open. More is drawn as current, because those pages are reached from it.

## Decided while drawing
1. **The phone clients list shows no money.** The Average check column doesn’t fit at 390, so the row keeps number · visits · last visit · next. The Owner sees the average check on the card (9b): Visits total and Average check. Admin and Specialist see no TMT on these frames.
2. **The phone Report menu stays a second bottom sheet** over the booking sheet, with the B-Calendar captions. Frame 4 shows the before-the-start case. The from-the-start case (four rows) is described in the caption, not drawn.
3. **A No-show booking (4b) has no footer.** The report box is the last block, and the booking can’t be moved or cancelled any more.
4. **The legend’s “Show cancelled” switch sits in the sheet footer** next to Got it, so every legend line fits on one screen. Status descriptions are 12/16 muted, as on B-Calendar.
5. **Reviews filter chips scroll in one line** instead of wrapping, so the first review shows above the fold. Sort is a full-width select under them. As on B-Reviews, “Not answered · 4” shows both as the summary pill and as a chip.
6. **The landing hero buttons stack full width** (Start free, then Try the business app). The header keeps a small Start free and the menu. The calendar mock is reduced to a request card + four blocks.
7. **The Team frame shows the chip row scrolled to Team**, so the current chip is visible. The member sheet (11b) opens from a row tap or from “…” → Access level. “…” opens an action sheet: Edit · Hours and time off · Access level · Remove from the team.
8. **Leýli’s “Doesn’t use the app” pill shows on her team row.** In the member sheet, “Uses the app” is off with B-Team’s two info lines. No SMS: the invite waits until she signs in.
9. **Settings and Reviews are reached from More** (no tab of their own). The More tab stays current there and its aria says which page is open.
10. **Client rows use B-Clients’ first 8 rows** (owner, sorted by last visit) with the same numbers, tags and timers. Gözel A. has 1 visit, +993 65 73 26 41 and the request timer 21 min. Team rows are B-Team’s five specialists, in client order.
11. **Sheet height and radius follow DS · Patterns** (max 92 %, radius 16), including the round-2 frames.
