# English copy for the design-system boards — design pass v0.6, round 1 (19.10.2026)

Ýeral is now built **English first** and isn't bound to one country; each market adds its own languages (Turkmenistan: Turkmen + Russian). The Turkmen drawn so far stays the reference for the Turkmen translation. Tokens, colours, type, spacing, radii and icons don't change.

## 1. Owner's words (use exactly)
- **Statuses** (icon + label, never a dot alone): Pending · Confirmed · Completed · Cancelled · No-show.
- **Who cancelled:** You cancelled · The business cancelled · The business declined (never confirmed) · No answer in time.
- **Actions:** Book · Send booking · Confirm · Decline · Move to another time · Change the time (title of the move flow) · Cancel booking · Undo · Book again · Report · Call · Directions.
- **Things:** booking, request, free time, service, specialist, any specialist, client, workspace, team, invite, permissions, internal status, busy elsewhere, hours, break, special days, day off, buffer, booking rules, instant confirmation, in review, calendar, pay at the place, free, near me, my bookings, favourites, upcoming · past, source: online · phone · walk-in.
- **Access levels:** Owner · Admin · Specialist.
- **Business product:** "Ýeral Business". **Brand:** the wordmark is exactly "Ýeral" (with Ý) in logos and copy; only the domain (yeral.app, business.yeral.app) and the SMS sender are written "Yeral".
- **Sample data** stays Turkmenistan's market (TMT, +993 phones, local business and people names) written the English way: `1,410 TMT` · `from 250 TMT` · rating `4.9` · `1.2 km`, `850 m`, `3 km` · `Thursday, 15 October` · `Today` · `Tomorrow` · `45 min` · `1 h 30 min` · `2 h` · 24-hour `14:30`, ranges `14:30–15:30` · `3 hours ago`. **Never a city name in copy** ("near me", "near you").
- **Prices:** fixed `80 TMT` · `from 250 TMT` · `Free` · hidden `Price at the place`. **No average bill anywhere** — remove every "≈ 150 TMT" / average-bill pill or row.

## 2. Voice (shown on DS · Foundations)
- "Sent", never "booked", until the business confirms: "Booking sent"; at an instant business "Booking confirmed".
- Errors say what to do next: "This time was just taken. Free times nearby:".
- No blame: a client limited to phone bookings sees "You can book this business by phone" + Call.
- No emoji, no new colours, one gradient main button per view, what a person can't use is hidden (not greyed).

## 3. House choices for words the list doesn't cover (use these on every board, so the boards agree)
**Spelling:** British English (colour, favourites, cancelled, centre, organise). Sentence case for buttons, labels, titles ("Send booking", not "Send Booking"). Numbers as digits. Times 24-hour.
