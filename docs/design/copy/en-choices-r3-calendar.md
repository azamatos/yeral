## Decided while drawing
1. **Money:** Owner sees everything plus the client’s average check. **Admin sees no TMT** on these pages (no day revenue line, no Price row, no prices on blocks, in the bookings Service column, in New booking’s service rows, total and footer, no amounts in Recent visits). **Specialist** sees the prices of her own bookings (her earnings) and her earnings line, but no client money (no average check, no amounts in Recent visits).
2. Report types already on a booking aren’t offered again (Came late, Rude behaviour); Other can repeat. Removing a No-show puts the booking back to Completed (or Confirmed if the visit hasn’t ended); removing any other report changes nothing else. Each report keeps its own 7 days.
3. The 24-hour window counts from the visit’s end time at the demo clock (yesterday 10:15–11:00 is already past it at 11:20; yesterday 17:00–17:30 is not).
4. A Specialist’s New booking lists and suggests only her own clients (with a booking with her); typing a full number still finds any saved client (she already has the number).
5. Demo clients aligned between the two pages (phones, visits, average checks from `business-demo.json` and B-Calendar); Gülälek N. shows her 1 no-show; Gözel A. 1 visit, +993 65 73 26 41, last visit 13 October.
6. A confirmed upcoming booking’s footer (Move to another time · Report · Cancel booking) is wider than the 480 drawer in English, so “Cancel booking” goes to a second line, right-aligned; the same happens with longer languages.
7. Fixed while checking: a round-2 CSS slip that hid the Break / Closed layer icons on the calendar; the banner text class clashed with the block-note class (`.bnt` → `.bnx`); on phones the banner button wraps under the text; the one-specialist week no longer widens the page with long labels.
