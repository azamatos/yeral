## Kept on purpose
- People and businesses as on DS · Business patterns (Aýna, Maral, Jeren, Leýli, Maýsa Annaýewa, Selbi Hojaýewa, clients; “Aýnanyň saç studiýasy”, “Glow Nails”); street “Bitarap Türkmenistan Avenue”.
- Page titles: “Calendar · Glow Studio”, “Bookings · Glow Studio” (the tab-title pattern on B-Shell); `lang="en"`. The pages show no address bar; suggested canvas frame titles: “Calendar · /w/glow-studio/calendar” and “Bookings · /w/glow-studio/bookings”.

## Formats
- Weekdays: long “Wednesday”; short “Wed” (`dShort`, block error, move popover: “Fri 16 · 10:00”, “Thu 17:00 → Fri 14:45”); two letters “We” only in the week grid, date strip and date picker (as on DS). Months in full in the calendar (“28 September”); compact in the bookings table (“Fri, 16 Oct”, history “28 Sep”).
- Durations “45 min · 1 h · 1 h 30 min · 2 h” (`durL` writes hours from 60 min). Money “1,410 TMT”, “from 250 TMT” (prefix, also in the new-booking total and footer).
- Plurals through a small `pl(n, word)` helper: “1 booking / 6 bookings”, “1 request”, “12 visits”, “2 no-shows”, “1 other time”, “6 rows”. The day summary under the toolbar gets its words from holes (`sumBkW`, `sumReqW`, `wkBkW`, `wkReqW`) so “1 booking” reads right.

## No SMS at the first release (brief §1)
Copy that promised an SMS now says “notify” or “My bookings” (DS · Business patterns still has the SMS wording in these places — it should follow):
- “Ýazylyş tassyklandy · müşderä SMS iberildi” → “Booking confirmed · the client is notified”; the same tail for “New time confirmed: Saturday, 17 October · 11:30 · …”, “Booking moved: 17:00 → 14:45 · …”, “Booking saved (· walk-in) · …”, “Booking cancelled · …”; bulk “N requests confirmed · the clients are notified”.
- “Haýyş ret edildi · müşderä sebäbi (we N başga wagt) iberildi” → “Request declined · the client sees the reason (and N other times)”; “Täze wagt ret edildi · … · müşderä sebäbi iberildi” → “New time declined · the booking stays at its time · the client sees the reason”; bulk “N requests declined · the clients see the reason”.
- Move-request box “… we müşderä SMS gider …” → “If you confirm, the booking moves to the new time and the client is notified. If you decline, it stays at its time.”
- Cancel modal lead “Müşderä SMS bilen habar bereris we sebäbini görkezeris.” → “The client is notified and sees the reason in My bookings.”
- New booking switch “Müşderä SMS bilen habar ber” → “Notify the client” (brief), caption “Sene, wagt we salgy iberiler” → “They see the date, time and address in My bookings”; move popover checkbox “Müşderä SMS iberilsin” → “Notify the client”.
- Phone hint for a new number: dropped the sentence “Müşderä SMS şu belgä gider.” → “This number isn’t saved yet — it will be added as a new client.”
