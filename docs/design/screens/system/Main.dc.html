Voice

Say what happened, then what to do next

01
“Sent” until the business confirms
Say “Booking sent”, never “booked”, until the business confirms. With instant confirmation: “Booking confirmed”.
Do
Booking sentView
Don’t
You’re booked!
02
Errors say what to do next
Name what happened in plain words and put the way forward right under it.
Do
This time was just taken. Free times nearby:
14:4515:0015:30
Don’t
Booking failed. Please try again later.
03
No blame
Offer what still works. Never tell people what they did wrong.
Do
You can book this business by phone Call
Don’t
You can’t book online because you missed 2 bookings.
04
Quiet by default
No emoji
No new colours — tokens only
One gradient main button per view
Can’t use it? Hidden, not greyed out
Later? Not drawn — no “coming soon”
No SMS promises — “the client is notified”
