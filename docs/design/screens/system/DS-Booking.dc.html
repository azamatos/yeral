Ýeral Web DS v0.6 · beyond beauty

Every booking is a place

Ýeral — from the Turkmen for “take a place” — works for anyone who sells their time: a barber’s chair, a dentist working from home, a restaurant table, a mat in a yoga class. One booking engine, four booking types — appointments ship first. The category a business picks decides its type, the search filters clients see and the services suggested at sign-up.

Where
At the business, at the client’s, or online
A dentist or tutor working from home can hide the exact address until the booking is confirmed — the map shows the district only.
Who
“Specialist” by default, renamed per category
Doctor for clinics, tutor for lessons, trainer for fitness, mechanic for auto. A solo business skips the step.
Same everywhere
Statuses, confirmation, reminders
Every type uses the five statuses and the same confirmation — by hand or instant. A gym can mix types: personal training first, classes later.
Money
Pay at the place
Prices are fixed (80 TMT), from (from 250 TMT), Free or hidden (Price at the place). No average bill. The booking summary keeps a hidden row for card deposits.
