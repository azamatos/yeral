Ýeral Web DS v0.3 · beyond beauty

Every booking is a place

Ýeral — “ýer al”, take a place — works for anyone who sells their time: a barber’s chair, a dentist working from home, a restaurant table, a mat in a yoga class. One booking engine, four booking types. The category a business picks decides its type, the search filters clients see and the services suggested at sign-up.

The list lives in the CMS (catalog structure module), so new categories ship without a release. Each category stores its icon, three names, booking types and a starter service list.
Where
At the business, at the client’s, or online
A dentist or tutor working from home can hide the exact address until the booking is confirmed — the map shows the district only.
Who
“Hünärmen” by default, renamed per category
Lukman for doctors, mugallym for tutors, tälimçi for trainers, ussa for mechanics. A solo business skips the step.
Same everywhere
Statuses, confirmation, reminders
Every type uses the five statuses and manual confirmation. A gym can mix types: classes plus personal training.
Money
Pay at the place, for now
Prices are fixed, “from” or none (restaurants show the average bill). The booking summary keeps a row for card deposits once online payment arrives.
