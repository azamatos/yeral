C2 · Tabs as panels

Each tab is its own panel

The owner: “Like YouTube: each tab is its own list. I don’t want to scroll or go back. Just let me tab and see About / Services without layout switching or scrolling.” Services · Specialists · Reviews · About sit right under the hero; each is a panel of its own.

At the top of the page

click Reviews

Scrolled into a panel

click About
About, from its top

The tab bar is stuck under the header. Switching jumps the page (instantly, no smooth scroll) so the new panel starts right under the tab bar — you never land in its middle.

A short panel

Services
click About
About (short)

The panel area keeps a min height = window − header − tab bar (dashed), so a short panel never pulls the page up and the footer never jumps into view.

How switching works

1Each tab is its own panel (role="tabpanel"), not an anchor on one long page. Services is the default; the others render when first opened.

2The URL follows the tab without a reload: /b/glow-studio?tab=reviews (history.replaceState — Back leaves the page, it doesn’t walk through tabs). “96 reviews” on the cover opens ?tab=reviews.

3Each panel keeps its state while you switch: the services search, “Show more” on reviews. Coming back to Services shows it as you left it, from its top.

4Keyboard: ← → move between tabs and open them, Home / End jump to the first / last; the focus ring wraps the tab box (A11).

5First open of Reviews or Specialists loads into the same min height with skeleton rows — the page never changes height while it loads.

6Below the panel area on every tab: “Places nearby” (4 cards) and the footer.

First idea · open questions for later

1Follow ≠ Favourite? Favourite saves a place to come back to; Follow asks for its news. If people don’t see the difference, merge them later: a favourite is followed by default and Updates has a mute switch.

2What counts as an update: a new service, an offer with an end date, a new specialist, special hours (holidays). The business posts them later from Settings; new services and special hours could post themselves.

3Where it shows: My account › Updates (avatar menu row with a count), newest first; offers past their date drop off. No push, SMS or Telegram in the first version.

4Signed out: Follow opens Log in first (like Favourite), then follows.
