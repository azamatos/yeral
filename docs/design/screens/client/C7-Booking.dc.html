Opens on Specialist. The service picked on the business page is already in the summary, so the first thing to do is the next real choice. The stepper keeps four steps and marks Service done (a link back to it).

A pick moves on. Specialist rows are buttons with a chevron, not radio cards: tap a row → it tints #efecfe and shows a check for 150 ms → Time opens. No Continue on this step, on desktop or 390.

What changed and why

  • The flow opens where the client is. A service picked on the business page opens the Specialist step with the service in the summary (/b/glow-studio/book?service=s1); services already picked on the business page all come along, and the list shows only people who do all of them; “Book” with nothing picked opens Services, then Specialist.
  • Single-pick steps move on by themselves. Specialist and Service rows are buttons with a chevron; a tap picks and opens the next step. Only Time and Confirm have a primary action.
  • Only the people who do the service are listed, “Anyone available” first (it books whoever is free at the chosen time), each with a labelled “Next free”.
  • The stepper keeps four steps in the order the client took them; done steps are links back with a violet check.

Trusted clients have 5 or more visits on Ýeral and no missed bookings in the last 12 months. With this on, their requests are confirmed at once, without the wait. Off until you turn it on.

The chip is the DS-Lists automatic tag (outline, icon, no ×) in completed tones: #ffffff fill, 1px #bfe6d1, #066a41 text and shield-check icon; 24 in tables and the drawer, 28 on the card.

It sorts first among the tags, can’t be removed, and the Tag filter lists it under Automatic with a count. The request drawer shows it in the client card, so the team can answer a trusted client’s request quickly.

What changed and why

  • Trusted = 5 or more completed visits on Ýeral and no no-show in the last 12 months. One mark across all businesses, like a verified badge.
  • Completed tones and a shield-check icon, drawn as the DS-Lists automatic tag: the positive twin of Reported.
  • The client sees it under the name in Account and on the confirm step, with a card that says what it means, how far they are (“3 of 5 visits”) and why it paused.
  • The business sees it on the client’s row, card and booking drawer; a place that confirms by hand can switch on “Confirm trusted clients at once”.
