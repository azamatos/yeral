What changed and why

1One bar, three real fields. The bordered card that stacked WHAT? / WHERE? / WHEN? over bare inputs is gone: one raised 72 px bar with icons, a 12/16 label and a 16 px field in each segment, and the Search button inside it.

2Focus you can see. The active segment lifts into a white pill with the v0.7 input focus (border #5a3ff0 + #efecfe halo) while the rest of the bar dims, so it is always clear where you type.

3Suggestions in a panel under the bar. The Where list no longer pushes over the button or gets cut: every panel opens 8 px below the bar, r 16, raised, max 420 px, and scrolls inside.

4What helps before you type. Popular = the 11 launch categories; Recent searches; while typing, grouped Categories · Services · Businesses with the match in 600 and keyboard support.

5Where is honest about location. Near me first; cities, then districts and streets as you type; when location is off an Info callout says so and the list still works.

6When is not a native select. Quick chips, a month calendar and Time of day in one panel; the segment reads “Tomorrow · Evening”.

7Search is the page’s one Primary. Primary L inside the bar on Home; the compact header bar keeps the round ink button (round 3 rule).

8Nothing found has a way out. “No places for “hairx” near you” with a spelling suggestion, “Search further away” (Distance to any) and Clear search — not a blank list.

Sheet behaviour

Opens full screen from the bottom (no scrim), focus in the tapped field; the keyboard opens for What and Where, not for When.

Three fields, always visible (L 48: search · pin · calendar icons). Tapping another field switches the content below; values stay.

Picking a suggestion fills the field and moves to the next one (What → Where → When); picking a category or business on What can search at once.

Show 24 places is sticky above the keyboard and counts live (debounced 300 ms); it runs the search and closes the sheet. ‹ Back closes and keeps the values.

When gets a Clear (Tertiary L) next to the main button; it resets the day and the time of day to Any time.

Same data as desktop: SuggestionsPanel and WhenPicker are the same components with touch rows (48 / 64) and M chips.

What changed and why

1A card, not three loose controls. The 390 hero showed one field and two chips (“Near me”, “Any time”) that looked like filters. Now the same three rows as desktop, each with an icon, a label and its value.

2One full-screen search. Tapping a row opens a sheet with all three fields on top — no step-by-step wizard, so you can fix Where without starting again.

3Suggestions as full-width rows (48 / 64) with the same groups, counts and match weight as the desktop panel.

4When is touch-friendly: quick chips, a full-width calendar with 40 px days and Time of day chips; Clear and Show 24 places stay at the bottom.

Rules

Where the day lives. The day is part of the search: the header SearchBar’s When (Compact at 1440, the summary field on 390) opens the same WhenPicker as Home. There is no day control in the toolbar.

Why not a Segmented “Today · Tomorrow · This week”? It would be a second control for the same value, and three fixed days can’t show a date, “This weekend” or “Evening” chosen on Home — the toggle would show the wrong state after a home search.

Row 1 (M 40, 12 apart): Distance · Rating · Price · spacer · Sort: Recommended. Row 2 (32): active filters as chips · Clear all · “24 places” at the right (A5: the header query counts as the search, so the count is always shown).

Result card: photo 200 × 150 r 12 · name 18/24 600 + rating · “Beauty · 3.0 km” · StatusPill · service line · up to 3 TimeSlots S for the searched day + “+N” (Tertiary S → the business page at the time step). The whole card links to the business page; hover #f6f6f8 syncs with its map pin (ink).

Any time searches: the first chip of a later day gets the muted day prefix (“Thu 10:15”) from TimeSlots.

Category pages (/c/beauty) use the same toolbar with the category in What.

What changed and why

1One height. The 38 px segmented and the 40 px select are replaced by one M row: FilterButtons and the Sort menu are all 40 high with r 10, so tops and bottoms line up (A3).

2The day moves into the search. When is chosen once, in the header SearchBar, with the same picker as Home — no second day toggle that disagrees with it.

3Filters look like filters. Distance, Rating and Price are DS FilterButtons with counts in their menus; an active one tints and drops a removable chip on row 2 (A5).

4Sort says what it does. “Sort: Recommended” with the sort icon instead of a bare “Most popular” select.

5Digits sit still. Time chips use Geist with proportional figures (A1) and a fixed S size; “+32” is a real button.

6Cards say if it’s open. The StatusPill joins the card; category and distance are two short items; no Example badge.
