Client site map

Every page of yeral.app: its route, its artboard and the states it covers. “Designed” pages are drawn hi-fi on this canvas; “Pattern” pages are not drawn — build them from the named screen. Behaviour lives in docs/design/README.md; when a screen and the spec disagree, the screen wins on looks, the spec on behaviour.

Decisions

Settled while drawing round 3

Header search buttonThe compact query bar’s round search button is ink (#101014) on every page. Gradient stays for the page’s one primary action.
Service group chipsFilter the list (not jump-scroll), on the business page and booking step 1.
Slot conflict copy“Bu wagt ýaňy alyndy. Golaý boş wagtlar:” + 3 nearest slots.
Table party sizeChips 1–8 and 9+. 9+ disables sending and shows “9 we ondan köp adam bolsa, jaň ediň” + call link.
SMS code entry4 boxes, auto-submit on the 4th digit; “Tassykla” button stays as the visible fallback. autocomplete="one-time-code". 3 wrong tries → 60 s lock.
Login modal width400 px (not 480).
Closing a booking flow× and “Yza” on step 1 return to that business’s page /b/{slug}.
Booking detail on phonesBottom sheet over the list (deep link opens the sheet). Desktop: right drawer 480.
LocationNo location → banner “Ýakynyňyzdaky ýerleri görkezeliň” above popular places. Denied → IP city only inside Nirede?, distances hidden, sort by rating × reviews.
Danger colourSolid danger buttons use #b42318 (hover #912018) — white text 6.6:1. #d9412f stays for dots, borders and the No-show badge.
Cancelled upcoming bookingStays in Gelýän (greyed, Ýatyryldy badge) until the next page load, then moves to Geçen.
Search orderingNearest first by default; never show a slot earlier than now + business lead time.
Cover photoA business’s first photo is its card and row image everywhere (Barber Loft = barber chair).
