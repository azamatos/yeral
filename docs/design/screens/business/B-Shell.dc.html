Ýeral Biznes — workspace shell

Every business screen sits in this shell: sidebar 248 (workspace switcher, sections for the person's access level, Ýeral sahypaňyz, Kömek, user block), top bar 64 (title, global search ⌘K, one primary action), content padded 32. Copy this markup for every business artboard. One Ýeral account (phone + SMS, same as yeral.app) can belong to several workspaces with a different role in each; a workspace = one business at one address; a second location is simply another workspace. Desktop-first ≥1024; tablet 768–1023 → 72 px icon rail; under 768 → bottom tab bar (see B-Mobile). Login: B-Login (/tk/giris).
