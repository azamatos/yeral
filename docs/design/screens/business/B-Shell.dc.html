Ýeral Business — workspace shell

Every business screen sits in this shell: sidebar 248 (workspace switcher · Calendar · Bookings · Clients · Reviews · Settings · user block), top bar 64 (title + one primary action — no global search at the first release; lists keep their own search), content padded 32. First release: appointments only and one language (English), so no Language row and no language in addresses. One Ýeral account (phone + code, the same as yeral.app) can belong to several workspaces with a different access level in each; a workspace = one business at one address, named with its city (“Glow Studio · Ashgabat”). Addresses: business.yeral.app/w/{slug}/… · /login (B-Login) · /setup/{step}. Tablet 768–1023 → 72 px icon rail; under 768 → bottom tab bar (B-Mobile).
