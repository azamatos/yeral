What changed and why

1Status and source are two equal badges. The source now says who made the booking — “Booked online”, “Booked by phone” (the team, after a call), “Walk-in” — instead of a bare “By phone” that didn’t say whose phone.

2Internal status keeps its Select and loses the grey line. “Only your team sees it. It doesn’t change the booking status.” sits behind an ⓘ next to the label.

3One client card. The name is the link to the client’s card (“Open the client’s card” is gone); the phone is grouped with a copy button; visits, last visit and no-shows are labelled facts — no “3 visits · average check 150 TMT” chain and no money in the drawer.

4Call and Book again are twins: two Secondary M buttons, half the card each. The alert note is a NoteRow with its author and date, not an amber box with a caps label.

5Details read as labelled facts in two columns; the footer uses the v0.7 buttons (Cancel booking = Danger quiet, Report ▾ = Secondary) and changes with the status.

6390 is the same drawer as a bottom sheet with L controls; Cancel booking moves to the end of the sheet so the sticky footer keeps the two safe actions.
