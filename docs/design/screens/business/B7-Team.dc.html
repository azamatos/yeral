What changed and why

  • Answer by is its own column: a TimerPill that fits its text with 8 / 10 px sides (“12 min”, red at 15 min or less; “By 09:30 tomorrow” for a request that came in while closed). It no longer stretches across the cell above the buttons.
  • Answer holds two 32 px icon buttons on one line, 8 px apart — ✓ Confirm (tint) and ✕ Decline (outline), each with a tooltip and a full aria-label. Every row is 56 px, one line; long text truncates.
  • The “…” menu appears only where a row has two or more other actions (Today and Upcoming: Move to another time · Report… · Cancel booking). Requests have none — the row opens the request; Past (Report…) and Cancelled (Book again) get one icon button.
  • One filter bar (A5): Search · Specialist · Service · Date · Status · Source · Sort, all M 40, tabs on their own row; Export CSV is a Secondary M at the right (owner and admins). The native “All specialists / All services / All time” selects are gone.
  • Scale: Today, Upcoming and Past are grouped by day with sticky headers (“Tomorrow · Thu, 15 Oct · 56 bookings”); specialists show their full name (Glow has two Marals). The bulk bar is a white raised bar with v0.7 buttons.
  • 1024: icon rail; Specialist folds into the Service cell; Sort and Export become icon buttons with tooltips. 390: request cards with one-line facts, the timer top-right, Decline and Confirm as two equal M buttons.

What changed and why

  • No “Management” group. It was invented from “has no services” and looked like a fourth role. One table instead — Name · Access level · Takes bookings · Services · Hours · Status. Takes bookings (✓ / —) says it plainly: anyone with a service is bookable, the owner and admins too.
  • Names on one line: avatar + full name, truncating with a tooltip. The job title moves to the tooltip and the member page — no second line, so every row is 56.
  • Status is one pill: Active · Doesn’t use the app · Invited · until 21 Oct · Removed. Invites and removed people are rows of the same table, not separate sections; a removed row has one icon button, Reactivate. Filters: Search · Access level · Takes bookings; “Add a teammate” is the one Primary.
  • Permissions is a matrix with three equal columns and symbols only — ✓, —, Own, View — plus footnote numbers listed under the table. What an action covers sits behind an ⓘ in its row. No cell holds a sentence; nothing wraps.
  • Member page footer: “Remove from the team” is Danger quiet M on the left; Cancel · Save on the right.
  • 390: people as list items (name + access level, then job · services and a status pill when it isn’t Active); permissions as one card per action with Owner / Admin / Specialist rows and the card’s own footnote.
