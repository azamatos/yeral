For the build

Phone rules · under 768 px

  • Addresses. business.yeral.app/login · /setup/{step} · /w/glow-studio/calendar · /bookings · /clients · /reviews · /settings/{section}. No language in the path.
  • Tab bar 64 + safe-area inset: Calendar · Bookings · Clients · More (4 equal items, 63 px targets, 11/14 labels). Bookings count = requests this person may answer. More = Reviews · Settings + the account items; no Reports, no Help. No app banner, no store links.
  • Sheets. Every drawer, modal and menu becomes a bottom sheet: grab handle, max 92 %, own scroll, sticky footer; drag down, scrim, × or Esc closes; focus trapped. Sheets cover the tab bar, except More.
  • Calendar. Day view, one column (owner/admin pick with chips; a Specialist sees only her own). Tap an empty slot → New booking · Block time; the round + opens New booking. Long-press a block to move it. Busy elsewhere: plain reserved block over the time and its buffer, never any detail, not tappable.
  • Requests. The timer runs only while the business is open; outside hours: “By 09:30 tomorrow”. Report: No-show only from the visit’s start; Came late · Rude behaviour · Other any time; Remove report for 7 days.
  • No SMS at the first release. Login codes show on screen; copy says “notify” / “My bookings”, never “we sent an SMS”.
  • Touch. Targets ≥ 44 px (36 px chips and links get a 44 px hit area); inputs 16 px text; phone fields inputmode numeric with a fixed +993.
  • Hidden, not disabled. Anything a role can’t use isn’t rendered (staff chips, Settings, money, answer buttons).
