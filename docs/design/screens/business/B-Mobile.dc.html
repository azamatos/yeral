For the build

Phone rules · under 768 px

  • Addresses. business.yeral.app (landing, signed out) · /login · /setup/{step} · /w/glow-studio/calendar · /bookings · /clients · /clients/{id} · /reviews · /settings/{section}. No language in the path, no language picker anywhere (one language at the first release).
  • Tab bar 64 + safe-area inset: Calendar · Bookings · Clients · More (4 equal items, 63 px targets, 11/14 labels). Bookings count = requests this person may answer. More = Reviews · Settings + the account items; no Language, Reports or Help. In Reviews and Settings, More stays current. No app banner, no store links.
  • Top bar. Calendar: workspace switcher + “?” (legend). Other pages: their title (+ back on a client card). No global search — Bookings and Clients keep their own. Switcher rows: access level · city (“Owner · Ashgabat”).
  • Sheets. Every drawer, modal and menu becomes a bottom sheet: grab handle, max 92 %, own scroll, sticky footer; drag down, scrim, × or Esc closes; focus trapped. Sheets cover the tab bar, except More. Toasts and the Settings save bar sit 12 px above the tab bar.
  • Calendar. Day view, one column (Owner and Admin pick with chips; a Specialist sees only her own). Tap an empty slot → New booking · Block time; the round + opens New booking. Busy elsewhere: plain reserved block, never any detail. Legend lines = B-Calendar’s.
  • Requests. The timer runs only while the business is open; a request that arrived while closed shows “By 09:30” (title “Answer by 09:30 tomorrow”). Headings count: “6 requests”, “1 request”.
  • Report. Before the visit starts the menu offers Rude behaviour and Other…; from the start also No-show and Came late (order: No-show · Came late · Rude behaviour · Other…). No-show only until 24 h after the visit ends, never after Came late; a type already reported isn’t offered again (Other can repeat). A No-show takes the menu away. Only no-shows count towards Phone only. Remove report for 7 days.
  • Money. Prices are booking information: everyone who sees a booking or request sees its price, the Admin included. “See revenue” covers only sums — revenue lines, client totals, the average check: the Owner sees all; a Specialist only her own earnings line; the Admin none.
  • No SMS at the first release. The login code shows on screen (label “Code”, “Get a new code”); copy says “notify” / “My bookings”; an invite waits until the person signs in with their number.
  • Words. “60 min”; “ · ” between date and time; “Cancel booking” cancels a booking, plain “Cancel” closes a form; “Time off”; Discard · Save; “Link and QR”.
  • Touch. Targets ≥ 44 px (36 px chips and links get a 44 px hit area); inputs 16 px text; phone fields inputmode numeric with a fixed +993.
  • Hidden, not disabled. Anything a role can’t use isn’t rendered (staff chips, Settings, sums, answer buttons, Add client for a Specialist).
