For the build

Phone rules · under 768 px

  • Navigation. Bottom tab bar 64 px + safe-area inset, 4 equal tabs (≥ 44 px targets): Senenama · Ýazylyşlar · Müşderiler · Köp. Ýazylyşlar badge = pending requests this person may answer (amber). Teswirler, Hasabatlar, Sazlamalar, Kömek, Ýeral sahypaňyz and the user menu live in Köp. 768–1023 → 72 px icon rail, ≥ 1024 → sidebar 248.
  • No app banner. The web app is complete on phones: no “get the app” banner and no store links anywhere. Native apps come later.
  • Workspace switcher sits in the Senenama header and at the top of Köp; it opens a bottom sheet. It is the only way to change workspace; switching reloads the whole app.
  • Sheets. Every desktop drawer and modal becomes a bottom sheet: grab handle, max 92 % height, own scroll, sticky footer for actions; drag down, scrim, × or Esc closes; focus is trapped and the page behind doesn’t scroll. Sheets cover the tab bar, except Köp.
  • Calendar. Day view only, one staff column. Owner/admin pick staff with chips (last choice remembered); Hünärmen sees only her own column and no chips. 1 h = 52 px; opens at now − 1 h. Swipe the grid for ±1 day, the week strip for ±1 week; “Oktýabr 2026” opens a month picker; “Şu gün” appears in that row when another day is open. Long-press a block to move it → confirm sheet with “Müşderä SMS iberilsin”. Week (load) view stays ≥ 768.
  • Blocks. Same D4 styles as desktop at 12 px icon size: icon first, then time and name; second line (service) from 36 px height; phone / alert / timer on the right. Cancelled and no-show don’t hold time: when a live booking overlaps them they take the right half.
  • Touch and input. All targets ≥ 44 px (36 px chips and links get a 44 px hit area). Inputs use 16 px text so iOS doesn’t zoom; phone fields inputmode tel with a fixed +993.
  • Toasts run full width with 12 px gutters, 12 px above the tab bar; the + lifts above them.
  • Hidden, not disabled. Anything a role can’t use is not rendered (staff chips, Sazlamalar, money, answer buttons).
