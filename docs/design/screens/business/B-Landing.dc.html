Features

From bookings to reviews — all your work in one place, on a computer or a phone.

  • Online booking 24/7

    Clients pick a free time and book themselves — at night and at weekends too, without a call.

  • Requests and confirmation

    Confirm each booking yourself or turn on instant confirmation.

  • Calendar and team

    Every specialist’s day on one screen. Drag a booking to move it to another time.

  • Client list

    Each client’s phone, visits and notes on one card.

  • Reviews

    After a visit, clients leave a review. You reply, and everyone sees your answer.

  • Link and QR

    Your own page with services, prices and free times. Share the link and put the QR code by your door.
