What changed and why

  1. One row instead of two, and every control in it is 40 high — the date, Day · Week, the people, Filters, Requests and New booking line up top and bottom.
  2. Today moves inside the date control as a small grey button: one click away, but it no longer looks like a filter. It greys out when today is already on screen.
  3. One people picker replaces “All staff / One specialist” and the native select: Everyone, one person or several, with search for 19 people and today’s count next to each name.
  4. Show cancelled and the statuses move into Filters (a switch row inside the menu), so the row no longer mixes a checkbox, a pill, buttons and a “?”.
  5. Requests is a normal Secondary button with an amber count and disappears at 0. The colour explanation moves to the bottom of Filters.
  6. The day summary is one quiet line under the row and no longer repeats the requests; money stays behind See revenue.

A week view you can read

The week is what the people picker chooses. One person → her 7 days as a time grid where every block says when, who and what. Everyone (or several) → a person × day table where each cell shows that day as a small timeline. Drawn on a busy week: Jeren has 38 bookings Monday to Saturday; the business has 305.

Seven columns don’t fit a phone, so the week is a list: a day head (date, Today, count, amber request count) that sticks while you scroll, then that day’s bookings in time order.

Each row: start over end time on the left (tabular), then a card in the booking’s status tint with the status icon, the client and “service · length”; a request carries its time left, a phone booking the phone icon. Breaks, closed time and busy elsewhere are grey rows; a day off is a day head with one “Day off” row; the now line sits between rows.

Tap a card → the booking sheet; tap a day in the strip → the list jumps to that day; Day in the Segmented → that day’s grid.

What changed and why

  1. Every week block says when, who and what — “10:00–11:00 · Jahan Muradowa · Facial cleansing” — instead of a blank coloured box.
  2. Short bookings shrink in steps: 30 min shows the start and the client on one line; 15 min shows “10:15 Jahan M.” with a status dot. Hover or focus shows the full card; a click opens the booking.
  3. The week grid is 68 px per hour, so 09:00–20:00 fits the screen, and breaks, closed time, busy elsewhere, the day off and the now line look exactly like the day view.
  4. Everyone’s week replaces the count-and-percent matrix: each cell is a small timeline of that day plus the count and the first and last time, so busy and free hours show without opening a day.
  5. Hover a cell for that person’s bookings; click to open her day. On a phone the one-person week becomes a list of days.
