What changed and why
  1. A day is a list of times on one line — “09:00 – 13:00, 14:00 – 20:00”. The break is the gap between two times: no Break row, no Break label, no second level. The owner: “a basic user won’t understand the pattern or idea.”
  2. “Add break” is a Tertiary S button, visible at rest, at the end of the day’s first line. It splits the last time (13:00–14:00 by default) and puts the cursor in the new time. × (“Remove the break”) joins the two times again. Up to 3 times a day.
  3. Times are TimeFields (DS-Controls A2): typed or picked in 15-min steps, the list opens on the value; no native selects.
  4. Problems show on their row — overlap, ends before it starts, outside Glow Studio’s hours — with the fix in the message. Save waits until they’re fixed.
  5. Each card header reads the week in one line (“Mon–Fri 09:00–13:00, 14:00–20:00 · Sat 10:00–16:00 · Sun closed”), so nobody has to read seven rows to check it.
  6. One component on every hours screen — Settings, Team (Business hours · Own shift as a Segmented M), special day, setup, phone. Where the day and two times don’t fit one line (480 drawer, 390), the times drop under the day name.
