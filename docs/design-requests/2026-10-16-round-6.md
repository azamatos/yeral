## A. Client: what happens to a booking after it's sent

**A1. Declined request: reason and offered times.** The business side is drawn (decline modal: reason chips, "Başga
wagt teklip et", up to 3 times, caption "Müşderi teklip edilen wagty bir basyşda kabul edip biler"). The client side is
text only (README §6 SMS). Draw, on the client: the row in "Meniň ýazylyşlarym" and the booking detail drawer for a
declined request, with the reason the client sees and the offered times as chips, and what happens after a tap.
Code today: taking an offered time after a decline creates a new booking that is confirmed at once; each declined
request turns into at most one booking, so after one time is taken the other chips go. Also draw the page the SMS link
opens (`yeral.app/b/{code}`) when the client isn't signed in.

**A2. Unanswered request (expired).** Code today: when the business's hold time runs out, the request is cancelled by
the system and the client gets 3 free times (SMS in README §6). Taking one sends a new request (pending again, unless
the business confirms instantly). Draw the system-cancel label (how it differs from cancelled by the business or by the
client), the 3 time chips, and the new request after a tap. Also draw the sentence README §4.4 promises on the "sent"
screen: "Jogap bermese, {N} minutdan soň size başga wagtlary hödürläris."

**A3. A move waiting for the business.** README §4.8: a move is a new request; the original stays confirmed until the
business answers. Neither side draws it. Code today: the original keeps its time and shows the new time as asked and
waiting; if the business says no, or doesn't answer in its hold time, the original stays as it was; a second move
replaces the first one that's still waiting; cancelling the original also withdraws the waiting move. At a business
that confirms instantly there is no waiting: the booking moves at once. Draw the client row and detail with a waiting
move, what the client sees after cancelling a booking that has one, and the business request card for a move
(B-Notifications already has the event "Müşderi wagtyny üýtgetdi").

**A4. Moving a table or class booking: the screens contradict each other.** Account-Bookings shows "Başga wagta
geçir" on every confirmed booking (Bag kafe YR-47990 and Aýdyň ýoga YR-47702 included), and Mobile tile 6 says "…mugt
üýtgedip ýa-da ýatyryp bilersiňiz". But the table and class rule texts only mention cancelling, and Booking-Table and
Booking-Class have no move mode. Code today: only appointments can be moved; tables and classes refuse. Decide:
(a) tables and classes can't be moved: remove the link for them and fix Mobile tile 6; or (b) they can: draw the move
mode of Booking-Table (party size and area kept?) and Booking-Class (another session of the same class?).

**A5. Moving an appointment: the move flow itself.** "Başga wagta geçir" opens the normal Booking flow, which has no
move mode. Code today: the services stay the same; the specialist stays unless the client picks another; if that
specialist is busy at the new time, any free specialist who does those services takes it. Draw the move mode: title,
what's prefilled or locked, whether the specialist step offers "Islendik hünärmen", and an "old time → new time" summary
before sending.

**A6. "Yzyna al" can fail.** Undo after a cancel (toast, greyed row). Code today: undo is possible for 5 minutes after
the client's own cancel, and it fails when the time was taken meanwhile, when a class has no seats left, when the visit
has already started, or when it was a request whose hold time ran out meanwhile. Draw these failures with their copy (one
shared state may do), and confirm or change the 5-minute window.

**A7. A client who may book this business only by phone.** The business side is drawn (B-Client "Diňe jaň arkaly
ýazylyp biler", the B-Settings rule after 2 no-shows). Code today: that client's online booking is refused, with one exception: a
time the business itself offered after declining can still be taken. States tile 05 covers only a business that paused
online booking for everyone. Draw what this client sees on that business page and in its booking flow (likely a
call-us state with "Jaň et"), worded without blaming the client, and confirm the exception.

## B. Prices

**B1. A hidden price on the client side.** B-Services: chip "Görkezme", preview "Müşderi görýär: baha
görkezilmeýär". No client screen has such a service, and the totals in Booking and Venue only handle "-den". Code
today: a hidden price is never shown or sent to the client; a total that mixes hidden and priced services shows as
"from" the priced sum (e.g. "40 TMT-den"); a total of hidden services only shows no amount. Draw a hidden-price service
row on Venue and in the booking flow, the total for a mixed selection and for a hidden-only one, and the price in the
booking detail.

## D. Tables and classes

**D1. The online party limit is a setting.** B-Tables lets the business set it (2–12, default 8; caption "{max+1} we
ondan köp adam bolsa, müşderä «Jaň ediň» diýip görkezýäris."), but Booking-Table draws chips 1–8 + "9+" and "9 we ondan
köp adam bolsa, jaň ediň". Show the client flow following the business's limit (e.g. a limit of 6 → chips 1–6 + "7+").

**D2. Party size in search.** Search rows show "Stol · 2 adam" (default 2) with no party control, while the API can
filter restaurants by party size. Should search have a party control for restaurants? If yes, draw it.

**D3. A phone reservation bigger than any one table.** In B-Tables the phone reservation has a 1–12 stepper and one
table ("Stol X — N ýer. Has uly stol saýlaň."); combining tables exists only in an existing reservation's drawer.
Should tables be combinable while creating the reservation?

## E. Hours and special days

**E1. Midnight.** B-Onboarding hour pickers go to "24:00"; B-Settings stops at "23:30" (business README §11:
06:00–23:30). Pick one. Code today: a day may end at 24:00 and prints "24:00". Show how a business that closes at
midnight looks to clients (Venue hours table, the "open now" line). "Open past midnight" stays open unless you design it.

**E2. Overlapping special days.** The "Aýratyn gün" modal checks past dates, reversed ranges and clashes with bookings,
not other special days. Code today, when several cover one date: a dated one beats a yearly one, then the narrower range
wins, then "Ýapyk" beats "Başga iş wagty". Draw a warning when a new special day overlaps an existing one (saying which
will apply), or change the rule.

**E3. Staff bookings in the past.** B-Calendar and B-Tables offer only now and later (`t < NOW` is off); walk-ins are
created at now. Code today: no back-dated bookings either, but staff may start at the current slot, so a walk-in at
11:20 with a 15-minute step can take 11:15, which the calendar doesn't offer (its first start is 11:30). Confirm there
are no back-dated bookings (recording a visit after it happened), and pick 11:15 or 11:30 for that walk-in.
