# Slice 4's shared ui, U1: Legend, StatusLegend, Meter (feat/ui-slice-4-u1)

- Changed (`@yeral/ui`; boards DS-Patterns → Status «Legend & status dot», «StatusLegend», «Progress & meters»,
  B-Calendar `legend` and `.lbar`, B-Mobile frame 8; COMPONENTS.md rows Display/Legend, Overlays/StatusLegend,
  Data/Meter):
  - **Legend** (`legend`, Display/Legend): a wrapping `ul` of keys, `items[{label, swatch?, icon?, value?}]`, `size` s
    (12/16 n-600, gap 6, 8 × 18 between keys, swatches 14 r4 with a 1 px line) / m (13/18, gap 8, 8 × 20; chart keys:
    a line 16 × 2 solid accent or dashed n-400, a 10 r3 swatch with a bold name and its value). `swatch` is
    `{kind: 'fill', color, line?, dashed?}` or `{kind: 'line', color?, dashed?}` with CSS values from the tokens
    (`var(--color-st-pending-bg)`); `icon` sits in the swatch's place («By phone»). Beside a chart with its own hidden
    data table the caller passes `aria-hidden`.
  - **StatusLegend** (`status-legend`, Overlays/StatusLegend): «Colours and symbols» — a ghost S 36 circle-help trigger
    (44 on phones, the group fill while open; `aria-haspopup="dialog"`), a popover 320 (pad 16, gap 16, title 15/20
    with its close button, scrolls past `100vh − 200`, footer line + «Got it» secondary S at the right) from 768 and
    the Drawer sheet under it (title 17/24, body gap 14, the sources in one line, the layers in two columns, an `extra`
    row under a line, «Got it» full width). Props `title`, `closeLabel`, `confirmLabel`, `sections[{title, items[{visual,
    text, wide?}], variant?: 'statuses' | 'keys' | 'inline', caption?}]`, `extra?`, `defaultOpen` (the first visit),
    `open` / `onOpenChange`, `onConfirm` («Got it»), `align`. The `statuses` rows are a 126 px column (a compact
    StatusBadge) + the meaning 12/16 n-600; `keys` rows are visual + words 13/18. Esc, outside and the buttons close it;
    the focus lands on the title and returns to the trigger. **LegendTile** (same module) draws the 22 r6 layer
    squares: `look` solid (break, closed time; a 12 icon) · hatch (buffer) · outline (outside hours, and busy
    elsewhere, a plain block with no icon since round 2) · pending (the «New time?» mark with a clock). Every word and
    visual comes from props: the booking entity supplies the statuses and their meanings, the sources, the layers,
    the timer rows and the internal statuses.
  - **Meter** (`meter`, Data/Meter): a presentational `span` (`role="img"` named by the caller's sentence) with `value`
    / `max`, `tone` accent · warning · neutral, `size` 3 (square ends) · 4 · 6 · 8, `surface` white (n-100 track) ·
    tint (white track) · grey (n-200 track, an n-50 panel), `segments[{value, tone}]` stacked in place of `value`
    (confirmed · pending · no-show; «full» is one segment at `max`); values clamp to 0…max, `max` 0 draws empty. A
    span so it can sit inside a button (the week's cells are buttons).
- Departures from COMPONENTS.md (the design's mapping): Legend's `swatch` is a tagged object (`kind`, `color`, `line`,
  `dashed`) rather than `{kind, color}`, so the dashed pending and cancelled fills draw their line; StatusLegend takes
  `closeLabel` and `onConfirm` beside `confirmLabel` and `defaultOpen` (the trigger is inside the component, the
  caller remembers «Got it»); Meter adds `surface` for the three tracks the boards draw (n-100, white, n-200) and a
  `label` sentence; the busy-elsewhere tile follows the round-2 rule (plain n-50 + n-200 line, no icon) over the row's
  older «dashed» note.
