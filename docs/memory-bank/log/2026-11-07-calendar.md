# 2026-11-07 · Calendar toolbar and week view (design v0.7 · B2, B3)

Branch `feat/calendar-toolbar-week`, board `docs/design/screens/business/B7-Calendar.dc.html`, rules `business/README.md` §29.2 (B2, B3).
The owner's complaints (04.11): too many tab filters plus a select bigger than the tabs, a whole filter for Today, a checkbox + pill +
button + «?» in one row, and a week view with nothing to read.

## What's there

- **Toolbar** (`pages/calendar/ui/calendar-toolbar.tsx`): one M 40 row that is the page's top bar — DateNav (one box: ‹ date › · Today
  as Tertiary S, greyed with «You're on today» on today; the date is `DatePicker look="nav"`), Day · Week, one **people picker**
  (`MultiSelect` with `summary` / `active`: «Everyone · 4», one name with her initial, «3 people»; Everyone is the first, ticked row),
  **Filters** popover (Show cancelled as a switch row, «What the colours mean» → the legend as a nested popover), **Requests** as a
  Secondary M with an amber count (hidden at 0; a click opens the most urgent), New booking the only primary. Under 1280 Filters and
  Requests are icons with a badge. The summary line is 13/18 and no longer repeats the requests.
- **Address:** `?staff=` carries the people picked, comma-separated, together with `?view=` (`dayOne` = the day of those people,
  `weekOne` one person's week, `weekAll` the team's). `?staff=` alone (no `view`) still means the phone's person, everyone on the desktop;
  old `?view=dayOne&staff=x` and `?view=weekOne&staff=x` links open as before. `peopleOf` / `viewFor` in `model/search.ts`.
- **One person's week** (`week-one.tsx`, 1 h = 68 px): `WeekBlock` (`entities/booking/ui/week-block.tsx`) by length — ≥ 45 min the
  time range, client and service; 30 min the start and client; 15 min a status dot, the start and «Jahan B.»; bookings at one time side by
  side (`lanesOf` in `model/grid.ts`); breaks, closed time and busy elsewhere as words; hover / focus 300 ms → `BookingHoverCard`
  (status + source badges, day and time, client and phone, service with length and price, Open); click → the drawer, the block ringed.
- **Team week** (`week-all.tsx`): a row per person, each cell a 10 px timeline on one scale (status tones, busy elsewhere grey), the
  count with the amber request count, the first–last time; «Day off» / «Time off» / «Closed · reason» / «No bookings · Works 09:00–20:00»;
  hover → the day's list in a card, click → her day. 168-px name column under 1280.
- `@yeral/ui`: `MultiSelect` `summary` + `active`, `DatePicker` `look="nav"`, `StatusLegend` `trigger`. The legend's first-visit auto-open
  and its localStorage flag are gone (the board retired them).
- `DayGrid` `only` is now a list of people (one person = the wide column).

## Traps

- `HoverCard` anchors with `PopoverAnchor asChild` on the block itself: a `display: contents` wrapper has no box, so Radix placed the
  card at 0,0. A dialog (Radix popover content) needs an `aria-label` or axe fails every story in the file.
- axe's `label-content-name-mismatch`: a button whose visible text («Requests 2») isn't in its `aria-label` fails; the name is now the
  visible text plus an sr-only « — open the most urgent».
- Hover cards fade in: check their text with `waitFor(() => expect(...).toBeVisible())`.
- The `WeekBusy` story's «Closed · Independence Day» check only runs when `HOLIDAY` (today + 2) falls in this week.

## Left

- Filters: the status checkboxes with counts (the api has no status filter yet); the cancelled count beside Show cancelled.
- 390: the «Whose calendar» sheet (the phone keeps the chip row), the week as a day-grouped list (the phone still shows the 7-column grid).
- The team week's job title under the name (not in the view), the day heads' request count in amber on the team week, DatePicker keyboard
  shortcuts (← → T), drag to move in the week.
- Stories for the new `@yeral/ui` props on their own boards (the calendar stories cover them).
