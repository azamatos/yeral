# Photos behind a storage port (feat/p2-photos)

- Asked by the design/build session (web-b2) for the name step and B-12; book F-084, F-101, Q-115, B-37 (chapter 9
  how-row, 21.10.2026, Claude (delegated)). Stacked on the setup PR (migration 0032 after its 0031).
  - `ports/storage.ts`: `StoragePort` (`put`, `get`, `remove`), `diskStorage(dir)` and `memoryStorage()`; keys
    `up/<folder>/<token>.<jpg|png|webp>` only (`typeOfKey`), so nothing outside the folder can be named.
  - Config `MEDIA_DIR` (`config.mediaDir`), `Deps.storage`; `GET /media/up/*` serves them, kept by browsers.
  - Domain `@yeral/domain/photos`: `PHOTO_MAX_BYTES`, `GALLERY_MAX`, `photoKind()` (by the first bytes).
  - `modules/media/photos.ts`: `savePhoto`, `dropPhoto`, `photoIdOf`.
  - The gallery (`settings/photos.ts`): `GET`/`POST /w/{slug}/settings/photos`, `DELETE …/{id}`, `PUT …/order`;
    `gallery_full` (tk, ru, en).
  - A person's photo (`me/photo.ts`): `PUT`/`DELETE /v1/me/photo`, `me.photo`; migration 0032 `users.photo`.
  - A team member's picture (`team/photo.ts`): `PUT`/`DELETE /w/{slug}/team/{staffId}/photo` (owner);
    `teamMemberSchema.photo`.
  - Contract `photoUploadSchema` (`z.file()`, told to OpenAPI as a binary string), `gallerySchema`,
    `galleryOrderSchema`, `galleryPhotoParamSchema`.
  - Infra: the api image makes `/data/media` for `node`; the server's and the local stack's compose mount a `media`
    volume there; `apps/api/data/` is ignored.
  - sdk `settings.gallery/addPhoto/removePhoto/orderPhotos`, `me.setPhoto/removePhoto`, `team.setPhoto/removePhoto`.
- Tests: `media/photos.test.ts` (the gallery added, served, ordered as the page's cover, refused when not a photo or
  too big, taken away from the store, 20 at most with the refused one not kept, the settings' people only; a
  person's photo replaced and removed; a team member's picture by the owner, an administrator 403),
  `ports/storage.test.ts`, `photos.test.ts` (domain).
- Review fixes (the PR's review, 15 findings):
  - Caddy takes 11 MB bodies (server and local stack); `PHOTO_MAX_BYTES` is 10,000,000, the edge's 10 MB.
  - `cleanPhoto()` (`@yeral/domain/photos`): JPEG segments without EXIF (an orientation-only EXIF kept), XMP, IPTC,
    comments, other APPn and what follows the image's end; PNG without eXIf, tEXt, iTXt, zTXt, tIME; WebP without EXIF
    and XMP, its VP8X flags cleared. Checked on real files: they still decode (sips, dwebp) and ImageIO still reads a
    JPEG's orientation. Anything cut short is `wrong_type`.
  - `releaseAccount` returns the photos that go with the account (its own; a deleted draft's gallery and team
    pictures); account deletion and both "Is this you?" paths drop them after the commit.
  - `uploadGuard` (route middleware, before the body is parsed): signed in, `UPLOADS_PER_ACCOUNT` 60 and
    `UPLOADS_PER_ADDRESS` 120 an hour.
  - `replacePhoto()`: the person's photo and a team member's picture lock their row, write, and drop the replaced
    file after the commit; a failed write drops the new one. A team row not found is refused before anything's kept.
  - `teamPhotos()` (`team/service.ts`): the team list and the calendar's columns fall back to the account photo; the
    public page shows only the owner's picture (as with names, B-12's edge case). A person's photo change tells the
    teams where it shows (`team.changed`).
  - sdk: `team.changed` and the team mutations refresh the business page too (it shows the team's pictures).
  - Uploads store `alt: {}`; `photoOf(deps, photo, lang, fallback)` reads the current name (business, specialist,
    person) for one without its own text.
  - `formBody()` next to `jsonBody()`; the dead `?? MARKET.defaultLanguage` and a duplicate import gone; `gallery_full`
    checked before the photo is written (and again under the lock).
  - Kept: the upload is read into memory (a 10 MB cap; three copies at most) and `/media/up/*` reads the whole file;
    Caddy could serve the volume itself later. No orphan sweep: every path that removes a row with a photo drops it
    (the seed's truncate only runs on development data). The contract and migration ride in this PR, stacked, as the
    design/build session asked for them together.
  - systemPatterns' module map: `media`, `setup`, `ports/storage`.
- Follow-ups: the business logo (not in the schema yet); the nightly backup takes the `media` volume (step 7); a
  setup draft's photos come with the profile step once the wizard draws it; an alert on the disk's free space (step 7).
