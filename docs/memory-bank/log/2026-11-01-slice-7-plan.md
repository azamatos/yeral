Book F-034, F-033, F-200, F-242, Q-004, Q-033, Q-036. The example businesses are live and the demo's api is built, so what's left
is the pages, the words that point at them, and the way into the demo. Then P5.

## Decisions (Claude, delegated)

- The pages' texts live in `apps/client/messages/pages.en.json`, loaded by those routes alone, so the words don't weigh on every
  page; a market adds its language with another file.
- Who runs Ýeral comes from `MARKET.legal` (the owner, 01.11), its address from `site.support.email`, so no text names either.
- The policy says plainly what the first launch is: a demo with made-up businesses, the sign-in code on the screen, no SMS, a server
  in the EU, nothing real to put in. The Turkmenistan launch gets its own text from a lawyer there (Q-033).
- `/try` lives on the business app because the demo's cookie is read only for its Origin (`<prefix>.demo`, ADR 0019).
- The business landing page (B-Landing) stays out: chapter 10's slice 7 doesn't name it, and `/` goes to the sign-in as it does now.
