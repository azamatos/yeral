# Slice 3's shared ui, U1: Pill, TimerPill, StatusDot, ExampleBadge (feat/ui-pills)

- Changed (`@yeral/ui`, DS-Patterns → Status, COMPONENTS.md rows):
  - **Pill** (`pill`, Status/Pill): a plain span, `tone` neutral · slate · accent · success · warning · danger ·
    outline · ink (on a photo), `size` s 20 / m 24 / l 28, `icon` before the text (12, 14 on l). Tags, not booking
    statuses (StatusBadge) nor buttons (Chip). `pillVariants` exported.
  - **TimerPill** (`timer-pill`, Status/TimerPill): `until` (a Date or epoch ms), `format(minutes)` and
    `label(minutes)` from the caller («18 min», «18 min left to answer»), `left(now)` for a hold that counts only while
    the business is open (the screen passes the open minutes left from `@yeral/domain`; by default the clock's minutes
    to `until`), `hotBelowMin` 15 (danger ink only), `size` m 26 / s 18 (white, on a warning tint), `by: { text, label }`
    for a request that arrived while closed («By 09:30», «Answer by 09:30 tomorrow»); `role="timer"` named by the
    sentence. Every timer on a page counts on one clock, `useClock()` from `@yeral/ui/clock` (to 15 s; the sentence
    beside a timer reads it too, so they agree); `now` fixes the time for a story or a page drawn on the server.
  - **StatusDot** (`status-dot`, Status/StatusDot): an 8 px dot and the label 14/20 600 in the tone's ink, success ·
    warning · neutral (slate) · muted, `detail` in n-600 after a decorative «·».
  - **ExampleBadge** (`example-badge`, Status/ExampleBadge): the neutral Pill with a flask, s (rows, pins) / m (cards,
    the page header); `ExampleIcon` (14, stroke 1.75) for a map pin. The word comes from the caller. A card or row is
    named by its words with the badge after the name in its markup and a hidden comma between («Glow Studio,
    Example»): an `aria-label` over what's seen («Glow Studio, example. …», as the board writes it) trips axe's
    label-content rule; a map pin, whose words are the name, says «Glow Studio, example».
- Departures from COMPONENTS.md (the design's mapping): StatusDot takes its label as children (not `label`);
  TimerPill's `until` takes epoch ms too and gains `left`; the pin's padding (0 10 0 8, gap 4) is the map's to set
  (lane C), not a MapPin variant yet.
- Stories (named as the boards list them: Pill Tones, Sizes, WithIcon; StatusDot Success, Warning, Neutral, Muted;
  TimerPill Default, Hot, Small, ArrivedWhileClosed, OpenMinutes, TimeUp; ExampleBadge Sizes, OnCard, OnSearchRow,
  OnPageHeader, OnMapPin), Russian-length words for each; 22 pass in Chromium (axe included).
- Follow-ups: the business app's own pills move onto these: `features/edit-hours/ui/affected-bookings.tsx` (its timer
  pill and tags), the Team list's level tag (`settings-team-page.tsx`), the special days' «Every year» tag.
