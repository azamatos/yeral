# Slice 3's shared ui, U1: Pill, TimerPill, StatusDot, ExampleBadge (feat/ui-pills)

- Changed (`@yeral/ui`, DS-Patterns → Status, COMPONENTS.md rows):
  - **Pill** (`pill`, Status/Pill): a plain span, `tone` neutral · slate · accent · success · warning · danger ·
    outline · ink (on a photo), `size` s 20 / m 24 / l 28, `icon` before the text (12, 14 on l). Tags, not booking
    statuses (StatusBadge) nor buttons (Chip). `pillVariants` exported.
  - **TimerPill** (`timer-pill`, Status/TimerPill): `until` (epoch ms), `format(minutes)` and `label(minutes)` from the
    caller («18 min», «18 min left to answer»), `hotBelowMin` 15 (danger ink only), `size` m 26 / s 18 (white, on a
    warning tint), `by: { text, label }` for a request that arrived while closed («By 09:30», «Answer by 09:30
    tomorrow»); `role="timer"` named by the sentence. Every timer on a page draws again at each new minute on one
    shared clock (`lib/use-minute.ts`, `useMinute()`); `now` fixes the time for a story.
  - **StatusDot** (`status-dot`, Status/StatusDot): an 8 px dot and the label 14/20 600 in the tone's ink, success ·
    warning · neutral (slate) · muted, `detail` in n-600 after a decorative «·».
  - **ExampleBadge** (`example-badge`, Status/ExampleBadge): the neutral Pill with a flask, s (rows, pins) / m (cards,
    the page header); `ExampleIcon` for a map pin. The word comes from the caller. A card or row named by its words
    puts the badge after the name in its markup (axe's label-content rule: «Glow Studio Example»); a pin named
    «Glow Studio, example» shows only the flask.
- Stories: every tone and size, the timer's hot, small, closed and time-up forms, the badge on a card, a search row,
  the page header and a map pin, Russian-length words for each; 17 pass in Chromium (axe included).
- Follow-ups: the business app's own pills move onto these: `features/edit-hours/ui/affected-bookings.tsx` (its timer
  pill and tags), the Team list's level tag (`settings-team-page.tsx`), the special days' «Every year» tag.
