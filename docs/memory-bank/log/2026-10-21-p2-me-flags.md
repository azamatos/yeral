# Two fields the apps asked for (feat/p2-me-flags)

- Asked by the design/build session (web-b2) for the client profile, B-13 and the invite card (B-08).
- `meSchema.canChangePhone` (`toMe(db, user, codeOnScreen)`): false while `SIGN_IN_CODE_ON_SCREEN` is on, when
  `/me/phone/code` answers `phone_change_unavailable`.
- `inviteCardSchema.requestAnswerers` (`inviteCards`): the business's "who answers requests" (`desk | each | both`;
  `both` when it has no rules row yet, the rules' default); on `GET /v1/me/invites` and `GET /v1/invites/{token}`.
- The business app's login fakes got the two fields (`entry.test.ts`, `login-page.stories.tsx`) so its types pass.
- Tests: `me.test.ts`, `team.test.ts`.
