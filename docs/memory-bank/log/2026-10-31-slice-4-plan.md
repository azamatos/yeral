## Decisions needed from the owner

- **D1 · What the app notifies, and how.** A browser can show a notification from the business app only while a tab
  of the workspace is open; while it's closed, notifying needs "web push" (a service worker subscription, a key pair,
  a per-device endpoint on the api), which the book leaves to the native apps. There is no notification settings page
  at the first release. Options: **A (recommended)** — three things: a new request, a client asking to change a time,
  and a request five minutes from running out. In front, a toast with a soft sound; in the background, a browser
  notification with the sound, which opens the request when clicked. The shell asks for the permission once with the
  drawn banner («Turn on notifications to hear new requests» + Allow) and says when it's blocked. Everything else
  (a client cancelling, a new review, a colleague's booking, the morning summary) waits for the settings page, as
  the book decided. **B** — also web push while the app is closed: about two more PRs, nothing drawn, and on iPhones
  only from an installed app. **C** — toasts only, no browser notifications for the demo. Why A: it's what the design
  drew and what someone with the app open on the desk needs; the demo's visitors sit at desktops.
- **D2 · The installable app.** Options: **A (recommended)** — a web app manifest with Ýeral's icons and a small
  service worker that caches only the app's own files: Chrome, Edge and Android offer «Install», an iPhone's «Add to
  Home Screen» opens it full-screen without the browser bar, and a lost connection shows «You're offline» instead of
  a blank page. No data is cached, no push, and no banner of our own asking to install (the design has none).
  **B** — the manifest and icons only: installs on Android and desktop Chrome, no offline page. **C** — skip it for
  the demo. Why A: it's what "installable" means on every phone, it's one well-known library (`vite-plugin-pwa`,
  rule 10a), and a cached shell opens instantly on a phone; the risk, a stale app after a deploy, is handled by
  reloading on the next open.
- **D3 · Moving a booking by dragging.** Options: **A (recommended)** — no dragging in slice 4: «Move to another
  time» in the booking drawer turns the grid into move mode; a click on the new slot (or a «nearest free time» chip)
  shows the ghost, a popover confirms «14:30 → 16:00, Aýna · Notify the client»; the same on a phone. Dragging comes
  as its own later PR once the calendar is in daily use (a drag library, pointer and keyboard, 15-minute snapping,
  telling a drag from a scroll on a phone). **B** — dragging now, in the move PR (it grows to L, and the phone is the
  hard part). Why A: the same result in two clicks, it works on phones, and the slice stays one screen per PR.
