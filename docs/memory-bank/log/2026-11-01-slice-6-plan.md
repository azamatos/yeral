Book F-178, F-179, F-180, F-238, Q-010–Q-012, Q-213, Q-261, Q-262, Q-274–Q-276, chapter 3 "The Ýeral team". The api is built
(`/v1/admin/…`, 22 paths, ADR 0026) and the contract is `packages/contract/src/admin.ts`, so this is a new app, `apps/cms`,

- A3 reported reviews and the team: `/reviews` with the 48-hour line, clear and remove; `/team` with the invite and its setup
  link shown once, reset and disable.
- A4 the stack and the slice's end: `cms.Dockerfile` (or `business.Dockerfile` taught the app), the two Compose files, the
  Caddy sites with their CSP, CI's checks, `e2e/tests/admin.spec.ts`, the deploy, the owner's account.

## Decisions (Claude, delegated)

- One language, as the first launch: English from `apps/cms/messages/en.json`, no language switch. The app takes the same
  i18n setup as the business app, so a market can add its languages.
- No service worker and no live channel: the team works at a desk, and the events stream belongs to user sessions, not the
  team's own cookie (path `/v1/admin`). Lists refetch on focus.
- The sdk's admin calls live in `packages/sdk/src/admin.ts`, added to `createSdk` as `sdk.admin`: `sdk.ts` is already past
  1200 lines.
- Verifying is refused while the sign-in code shows on the screen (`verify_unavailable`, ADR 0023). The button stays and says
  why, read from the same public flag the sign-in reads, so the demo doesn't look broken.
- One role (Q-011): every account sees every screen, so the app has no permission checks of its own.
- Phones: a desk tool, so it works down to 390 without a design of its own: the sidebar becomes a row of tabs on top, tables
  scroll inside their frame.
