# A move request says whether it fits (slice 3 gap 4, feat/move-fits)

- Changed: `GET /v1/w/{slug}/requests` items gain `fits` (contract `requestSchema.fits`): for a move, whether it can be
  confirmed now, B-21's line ("Maral is free at the new time — you can confirm." or not, then Confirm is off); null for
  a new request. One rule, `moveProblem` (`requests/service.ts`): the business is open at the new time (a day closed
  since) and its specialist works then and isn't blocked (a day off, a new shift, blocked time, gone from the team); the
  confirm refuses by the same rule (`cannot_move` with its `problem`), so the line and the button never disagree. The
  calendar page's stories (B1) gained `fits` in their two mocks.
- API test (CI): a move fits while the day is open; new requests say null; once the business closes that Friday it
  doesn't fit and its confirm is refused (409 `cannot_move`, `outside_hours`).

  running is held" failed once on #224: an earlier test's event, held a while by other test files' transactions, showed
  up in the read that expected none. It now watches only its own event (the ids after the newest before it) and waits
  on the condition (up to 30 s, the test 40 s) instead of a 5 s window.
