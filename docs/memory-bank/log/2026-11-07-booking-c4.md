## Decisions
- The amber row says only «Too short for {duration}.»: the api's free times don't say when the specialist is busy, so
  the board's «Jeren is busy from 12:45» waits for an api field (none added).
- «Pay at the place · cash or card» on the confirm card isn't shown: nothing in the data says a business takes cards.
- «Is this you?» (an account unused for 6 months, SMS codes only) isn't drawn inline: it opens the site's login modal.
- A service already in the booking is a disabled option in the list; the × on its summary row is the way to take it off.
- The specialist step lists only who does every picked service (the board), no greyed rows; the lead says how many.
