# StatTile, SectionHeader, TagInput and BulkBar in `@yeral/ui` (P4 slice 4, PR U2; feat/ui-slice-4-u2)

Slice 4's shared lane U2 (`log/2026-10-31-slice-4-plan.md` → "PRs, three lanes"): the four domain-free pieces the
bookings list's requests tab (B9), the clients list (B10) and the client card (B11) need. Every value comes from
DS-Patterns (`docs/design/screens/system/DS-Patterns.dc.html`) and the business boards it cites; `COMPONENTS.md` is
the owner's export and stays as it is — the rows map to these files as below, and each component's docblock says
which board it comes from. No user-visible string lives in the package: words come in as props.

- **StatTile + StatRow + StatTileSkeleton** (`packages/ui/src/components/stat-tile`, Storybook Data/StatTile;
  `COMPONENTS.md` row "StatTile / StatRow"; board DS-Patterns 03 «Stat tile · L 32 (key figures) and M 22 (cards)»,
  `.y-stats .y-stat .y-tv .y-sv .y-tdr`). `StatTile`: `label` (11 caps n-500), `value` (tabular, nowrap; L 32/38
  700 −0.02em, 24/30 on phones; M 22/28 700 −0.015em), `delta?` (a node beside the caption: a `Pill` until
  DeltaPill exists; L only), `caption?` (13/18 n-600; truncates when alone), `size` `l` (gap 6) | `m` (gap 4), the
  rest on the `div` (`role="listitem"`). `StatRow`: `columns` 4 | 3, `loading` (the row says `aria-busy` and stays
  `role="list"`, so a known tile beside a skeleton is still a list item; name it with `aria-label`), 1 px n-200 dividers through the tiles' left border,
  padding 0 24, 2 × 2 under 768 with the third tile starting its line without a divider. `StatTileSkeleton` keeps the
  geometry (72 × 14, 90 × 38 or 28, 120 × 18). A value a role may not see is left out, never blanked: the row
  re-flows 4 → 3 (story HiddenValue, the toolbar's lock line stays the screen's). Stories: Large, Medium, WithDelta,
  HiddenValue, Loading, NoComparison, Phone, Russian (+30 % length).
- **SectionHeader** (`section-header`, Display/SectionHeader; board DS-Patterns 03 «Section header row», `.y-shrow
  .y-sh`, from B-Client Notes · Upcoming · History · Reports). Props: `title`, `as` `h2` (default) | `h3`, `titleId`
  (for a section's `aria-labelledby`), `count?` (13/18 n-600 beside the title), `action?` (pushed to the end: an S
  secondary `Button` «Add note» or a caption «Only the team sees them»); a 36-min-high row, gap 10, title 17/24 600
  −0.01em, wraps on narrow widths. Stories: Default, WithCount, WithAction, WithCaption, AsH3, Phone, Russian.
- **TagInput** (`tag-input`, Inputs/TagInput; board DS-Patterns «Tag input · toggle tags + create»: Toggle, Adding,
  AutoTags, from B-Clients' new-client modal and B-Client's edit modal). Composed from `Chip`, `inputClass`, `Button`
  and `Pill` (rule 10a: no tag library). Props: `options` (the team's tags, toggle chips with `aria-pressed`),
  `value` + `onChange(string[])` (a value not in `options` shows as a chip too), `automatic?` (`{label, tone?}[]`,
  Pill L, never buttons), `caption?` (13/18, wired as the group's `aria-describedby`), the words `addLabel` («tag»,
  after the + icon), `newTagLabel` (the field's name), `addButtonLabel` («Add»), `placeholder?`, `disabled?`. The
  «+ tag» chip opens a 36 × 180 pill field + S secondary Add: Enter adds (a tag that exists in any case is switched on
  instead of added twice; empty adds nothing), Esc cancels only the field, also inside a Modal or Drawer (Radix hears
  Esc on `document` in the capture phase, so the component claims it on `window` first while the field is open), the
  focus returns to the chip; a value in another case than its option (`vip`) is that option's chip. Inside `Field` the
  group takes the label (`aria-labelledby`) and `disabled`; elsewhere name it with `aria-label`. Stories: Toggle,
  Adding, Cancel, InModal, ValueInAnotherCase, Duplicate, AutoTags, Disabled, Phone, Russian.
- **BulkBar** (`bulk-bar`, Data/BulkBar; board DS-Patterns Table «bulk bar ink, radius 0 0 15 15 · disabled on ink =
  n-600 line, n-500 text» and Button «On ink · bulk bar, toast, move bar», from B-Bookings `.bulk .bk-w .bk-o
  .bk-x`). Props: `count` («2 selected», formatted by the caller), `clearLabel` + `onClear` (the × at the end, an
  `inverse-ghost` icon button), `children` (the actions as `Button` S `inverse-solid` for the one main action,
  `inverse-outline` beside it), the rest on the `div` (`role="region"` + `aria-live="polite"`: name it). Sticky to the
  viewport's bottom (z 5; 64 up on phones, above the tab bar), n-900, gap 12, padding 12 12 12 20, radius 0 0 15 15,
  wraps; under 768 its buttons grow to 44 with radius 12. Stories: Default, Disabled (nothing pending among the
  selected rows: Confirm and Decline off, Export CSV on), ExportOnly, Keyboard, Phone, Russian.
- **Button** gained the on-ink set from that board: `inverse-solid` (white, n-900 text, hover n-100),
  `inverse-outline` (n-600 line, white text, hover ink-btn-hover) and `inverse-ghost` (the clear ×); disabled on ink
  keeps the transparent fill with an n-600 line and n-500 text (the board's win over B-Bookings' `#3a3a42 / #8a8a94`).
  Story OnInk checks the disabled colours. `inverse-link` (toasts' a-200 link) waits for the toast that needs it.
- **Table** lost its own bar: `BulkBar`'s first draft in `table.tsx` and `BulkBarButton` are gone; `@yeral/ui/table`
  re-exports the new `BulkBar` so `COMPONENTS.md`'s "keep exports" holds, and Data/Table's BusinessBookings story
  uses it with on-ink `Button`s. Nothing in the apps imported either (checked).

Checks: `pnpm check` at the root; `pnpm test:stories` for the four new folders plus `table` and `button` (41 story
tests, axe included).

For the screen PRs:
- B9 (`features/bulk-handle-requests`, the requests tab): `BulkBar` from `@yeral/ui/bulk-bar` with
  `count={t('bookings.selected', {count})}`, `aria-label` (required by the type), `clearLabel`, `onClear`; inside it `Button` S
  `inverse-solid` Confirm, `inverse-outline` Decline (one reason) and Export CSV with the Download icon; disable
  Confirm and Decline when no selected row is pending; render only Export for a viewer who may not answer; don't
  render the bar at all for a Specialist. The bar goes last inside `TableFrame`, which now clips with `overflow-clip`:
  the corners keep the frame's radius and the bar sticks to the viewport's bottom (under `overflow-hidden` the frame
  was the scroll container and the bar sat at the table's foot, off-screen; the story InTallTable checks it). Don't
  put an `overflow-hidden` or `overflow-auto` ancestor between the bar and the page.
- B10 (`features/create-client`, the add-client modal) and B11 (the edit modal): `TagInput` inside a `Field
  label=…` with the team's tags as `options`; for an existing client pass `automatic` (Regular / New from the client's
  segment, tones neutral / success) with the caption; all five words from the app's messages.
- B11 (`pages/client`): ClientStats = `StatRow aria-label columns={4|3}` of `StatTile size="m"` (Visits · Average
  check · Last visit · No-shows); leave the average-check tile out without See revenue (`columns={3}`); captions and
  the formatted values from `useFmt`. Section rows (Notes with «Add note», Upcoming, History, Reports with its caption)
  = `SectionHeader` with `titleId` + `<section aria-labelledby>`.
- Analytics (later): the L row with `delta` from DeltaPill once it exists (`COMPONENTS.md` row DeltaPill, not built
  here); `StatTileSkeleton` ×3 or ×4 in a `StatRow loading`.

Follow-ups: DeltaPill (Status/DeltaPill) is still "new"; the StatTile stories draw the delta with `Pill tone=success`
+ ArrowUp until then. `Button` `inverse-link` with the toast that needs it.
