## Spacing, radii, elevation

- Spacing unit 4px: `p-4` = 16, `gap-6` = 24; section rhythm 48 / 64 / 96. Exact values from the screens, e.g. `px-4.5` = 18.
- Radii: `rounded-input` 8 · `rounded-s` 10 · `rounded-m` 12 · `rounded-l` 14 · `rounded-card` 16 · `rounded-pill`.
- Sizes: buttons 36 / 44 / 52, inputs 48, list and table rows 56 (dense 40), nav 72 (search header 80), sidebar 248 → rail 72.
- Layout: breakpoints ≥1440 · 1280 · 1024 · 768 · ≤767; grid 12 / 8 / 4; side margin 40 / 32 / 24 / 16; marketplace container 1200.
- Elevation: `shadow-raised` (popover, drawer, modal, toast) and `shadow-sheet` (phone sheets); nothing else casts a shadow.
